# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our standard

Be kind and constructive. Disagree with ideas, not with people. This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space of the project.

Welcome: respectful feedback, patience with beginners, credit for other people's work, and admitting mistakes.

Not welcome: harassment, insults or personal attacks, discrimination of any kind, publishing other people's private information, trolling, and anything you would not say face to face.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block repeat offenders.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately through the contact options on the maintainer's GitHub profile (<https://github.com/facunicedev>). Reports are handled with discretion.
